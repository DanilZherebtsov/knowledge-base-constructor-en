# State-rules body for research (question-lifecycle)

Inserted by wiring into base `state-rules.md`: replaces the S5 slot region ("## Structure") and refines rule 2b. Base rules 1, 3–7 come from base.

## Structure (7 sections)

1. **Stage** — where we are in the lifecycle and what the current goal is (scoping / systematic review / data collection / analysis / synthesis / write-up).
2. **Path to goal** — milestones from the current moment to the goal, in order.
3. **In progress now** — which questions are in flight right now (usually one or two). The question's working file — in `output/` (`q-NNN-...`).
4. **Next (1–2 weeks)** — the near-term agenda; one-liners here, no file until the question is taken into work. Beyond that — `BACKLOG.md`.
5. **Answered last week** — the buffer (rule 6 below).
6. **Blockers and risks** — what is in the way of the current work; risks outside it go to `BACKLOG.md`.
7. **Low-priority open questions** — a single pointer line to the "Open questions" section of `BACKLOG.md`; until it exists — `_empty_`.

**Granularity mirrors the horizon of understanding.** Early on, "Path to goal" is 2–3 lines in broad strokes ("map the field", "8 interviews", "review draft"). As it approaches, it gets broken down. Don't atomize prematurely — that is false precision.

## Rule 2b (moving the unit of work)

Once a question is answered and claims/synthesis have been extracted from it, Claude moves it from "In progress now" to "Answered last week".
